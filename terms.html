Using subbr

subbr is for adults (18 and over). Use it within the law. Don’t use it to harass, threaten or deceive anyone, or to hide or pass on anything illegal. You are responsible for what you send and who you send it to.

Your messages are yours, and so is the risk of losing them

Your friends, passwords and history are kept only on your phone; we have no copy. If you forget a password, lose or reset your phone, uninstall subbr or use Settings → Delete everything, they are gone for good, and we can’t recover them.

What subbr can and can’t promise

subbr encrypts your secret with standard, publicly reviewed methods (Argon2id and XChaCha20-Poly1305). Without the password, nobody can read it, but a careful inspector may be able to tell that something is probably hidden. Your safety also depends on things we don’t control: how strong your password is, who you share it with, and who can get into your phone. Chat apps may also shrink or change photos and videos, which can sometimes break the hidden message.

The purchase belongs to your Google account and comes back if you reinstall subbr with that account.
