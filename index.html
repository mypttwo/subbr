subbr

Hide a secret in plain sight.

subbr hides a private message inside something ordinary: a sentence, a photo or a short video. Send it with any app you like. Everyone else sees an everyday message; only your friend can reveal it.

Text, photos and videos

Hide your message in an everyday sentence, a photo or a short video. Photos survive WhatsApp’s compression.

Any app

Send it with WhatsApp, Telegram, email or any app you already use, or straight to your friend’s chat.

Only your friend can read it

The message is encrypted with a password you share with your friend. Without it, no one can read it.

How do you say “subbr”?

Say it like “subber” (SUB-er). It comes from subliminal: a message that sits under the surface, there for the one person meant to see it.

How it works

  • Add a friend once. Scan each other’s QR code when you’re together, or both type the same password when you’re apart.
  • Hide. Type your secret, pick the friend, and hide it in a sentence, a photo or a video.
  • Send. Send it with any app, or straight to your friend’s chat.
  • Reveal. Your friend copies the text, or shares the photo or video to subbr, and the secret appears.

Self-destruct timers

Show a message for 10 seconds, 30 seconds or a minute; let it open only once; or make it expire after an hour, a day or a week.

Privacy

  • Only someone with the password can read your messages. subbr uses strong, modern encryption (Argon2id and XChaCha20-Poly1305).
  • No internet permission. subbr can’t send anything anywhere on its own. You can check this on its Google Play page.
  • No account, no phone number, no email. No access to your contacts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Everything stays on your phone, encrypted. Your friends, passwords and history never leave it. You can also lock subbr with your fingerprint, face or screen lock.
  • The camera is used only to scan QR codes. Photos and videos are read only when you pick them.

An honest note: a careful inspector can tell that something is probably hidden, but without the password they can’t read it.
