subbr

Hide a secret in plain sight.

subbr hides a private message inside something ordinary: a sentence, a photo or a short video. Send it with any app you like. Everyone else sees an everyday message; only your friend can reveal it.

Text, photos and videos

Hide your message in an everyday sentence, a photo or a short video. Photos survive WhatsApp’s compression.

Any app

Send it with WhatsApp, Telegram, email or any app you already use, or straight to your friend’s chat.

Only your friend can read it

The message is encrypted with a key that only your phone and your friend’s phone have. Without it, no one can read it.

Messages that disappear

Show a secret for 10 seconds, let it open only once, or make it expire after an hour, a day or a week. After that, subbr can’t show it again, on either phone.

Who it’s for

  • Couples, friends and family who want a private line inside the chats they already use.
  • Anyone whose phone or chats others might look at. A hidden message looks like an everyday one, so it draws no attention.
  • Fun: secret notes, puzzles, a hidden message in a birthday photo.
  • People who like simple, private tools: subbr works offline and collects nothing.

How it works

  • Add a friend once. Scan each other’s QR code when you’re together, or send an invite link on WhatsApp or Telegram when you’re apart.
  • Hide. Type your secret, pick the friend, and hide it in a sentence, a photo or a video.
  • Send. Send it with any app, or straight to your friend’s chat.
  • Reveal. Your friend copies the text, or shares the photo or video to subbr, and the secret appears.

Features

  • Text, photos and videos. Hide in an everyday cover sentence (ready-made or your own), a photo, or a video (quick up to 15 seconds, or full quality up to a minute). Photos survive WhatsApp’s compression.
  • Disappearing messages. 10, 30 or 60 seconds on screen, open once, or expire after an hour, a day or a week.
  • Remembers your friends. Nothing to type: messages reveal by themselves. Each friend has a chat with your last 10 messages, stored encrypted.
  • Private by design. No account, no ads, no tracking, no internet permission. Lock subbr with your fingerprint, face or screen lock if you like.
  • Five languages: English, Arabic, Spanish, Hindi and Indonesian.

Why not just a secret chat?

  • No separate secret chat. Nothing shows that you’re having a private conversation: the message travels inside the chat you already have and looks ordinary.
  • Better than disappearing messages. Those can be read by anyone holding the phone while they last. A subbr message can’t be read at all without the key that only your two phones have.
  • More than a photo-hiding app. Text, photos and videos; friends remembered, so there’s nothing to type; and no ads.

Price

Revealing is always free. Your first 5 secrets are free, then a one-time unlock: no subscription, no ads.

Honest limits

  • Your friend needs subbr too, but receiving and revealing are free.
  • Timers run in subbr on your friend’s phone. The hidden message stays in the chat, but no one can read it without the key.
  • A careful inspector can tell that something is probably hidden, but without the key they can’t read it.
  • Android only for now.

Privacy

No account, and nothing leaves your phone.

How do you say “subbr”?

Say it like “subber” (SUB-er). It comes from subliminal: a message that sits under the surface, there for the one person meant to see it.
