subbr

One contact. One conversation. Any app.
And no one can tell.

Talk to a friend through whatever app you like: WhatsApp today, Telegram tomorrow, email next week. subbr keeps it all in one conversation, encrypted and hidden inside ordinary messages.

One contact, one conversation

Everything you and a friend send each other lands in one chat in subbr, whichever app it travelled through. One place to read it all.

Any app

subbr hands your message to the app you choose, and you pick again any time. It works with the apps you and your friends already use.

No one can tell

Your message is encrypted and hidden inside a harmless sentence, a photo or a short video. Everyone else sees something ordinary. Only your friend's subbr can reveal it.

How do you say “subbr”?

Say it like “subber” (SUB-er). It comes from subliminal: a message that sits under the surface, there for the one person meant to see it.

How it works

  • Add a friend once. Scan each other's QR code, or send an invite link. Each phone keeps its own key, so no password has to be shared.
  • Write and send. subbr hides the message and hands it to the app you choose.
  • Read. Your friend copies the message, or shares the photo or video to subbr, and reads it in your conversation.

Privacy

  • No account, no phone number, no email.
  • No subbr server sees your messages. They travel only through the apps you choose, hidden and encrypted.
  • Your key, contacts and chats stay on your phone, encrypted and locked with your PIN.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • The camera is used only to scan QR codes.
