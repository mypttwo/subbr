subbr

Secret messages that travel through any app

subbr encrypts a message and hides it inside something ordinary: a harmless sentence, a photo or a short video. You send that through the apps you already use, such as WhatsApp, Telegram or email. Everyone else sees the ordinary message. Your friend's subbr reveals the secret.

How it works

  • Add a friend once. Scan each other's QR code, or send an invite link. Each phone keeps its own key; no password has to be shared.
  • Write and send. subbr hides the message and hands it to the app you choose.
  • Read. Your friend copies the message, or shares the photo or video to subbr, and reads it there.

Privacy

  • No account, no phone number, no email.
  • No subbr server sees your messages. They travel only through the apps you choose, hidden and encrypted.
  • Your key, contacts and chats stay on your phone, encrypted and locked with your PIN.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • The camera is used only to scan QR codes.

A full privacy policy will be published here before subbr is available on Google Play.
