subbr

Hide a message in plain sight.

subbr puts a private message inside an ordinary sentence, photo or video. Send it with any app you like. Everyone else sees something ordinary; only a friend with your shared password can reveal it.

Looks ordinary

“See you Saturday at noon” can carry a completely different message. So can a holiday photo or a short video.

Any app

WhatsApp, Telegram, email, SMS: subbr hands the result to the app you choose, or straight to your friend's chat.

Nothing leaves your phone

No account, no internet access, no ads, no tracking. subbr can't send anything anywhere on its own.

How it works

  • Add a friend once. Together: one phone shows a QR code, the other scans it. Apart: you both type the same password, agreed on a call or in person.
  • Hide. Type your secret, choose the friend, and hide it in a sentence, a photo or a video. Optionally set a self-destruct timer.
  • Send. Share it with any app, or straight to your friend's WhatsApp, Telegram or email.
  • Reveal. Your friend copies the text, or shares the photo or video to subbr. subbr tries their saved passwords and shows the secret.

Help

How do I reveal a message?
Text: copy the whole message and tap Reveal from clipboard, or select it and choose Reveal with subbr. Photo or video: share it to subbr from WhatsApp, Telegram or your gallery.

It says “None of your saved passwords opens this”.
The message was hidden with a password you haven't saved. Enter it once; subbr can save it as a friend.

It says the message was damaged.
Part of it was lost, usually because only part of the text was copied. Copy the whole message and try again.

What do the timers do?
Show for 10 s, 30 s or 1 min: the secret disappears after that and opens only once. Open once: it can be revealed a single time. Expires in 1 hour, 1 day or 1 week: after that it won't open. The timer works in subbr on your friend's phone; the hidden message itself stays in the chat.

Quick or full-quality video?
Quick keeps up to 15 seconds at 15 frames a second and is ready in seconds. Full quality keeps up to 60 seconds at the full frame rate and takes about as long as the video, or longer.

Can I lock subbr?
Yes: Settings → Lock with fingerprint, face or screen lock. It's off by default.

Which languages?
English, Arabic and Spanish. subbr follows your phone's language, or choose one in Settings.

How do you say “subbr”?

Say it like “subber” (SUB-er). It comes from subliminal: a message that sits under the surface, there for the one person meant to see it.

Privacy in short

  • No account, no phone number, no email.
  • No internet permission: subbr has no way to send anything anywhere.
  • Your friends, passwords and history stay on your phone, encrypted.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking.
