Subber

Privacy policy

Subber is built so that we can’t see your messages, your contacts or who you talk to. This page explains exactly what the app does with data.

Effective: [date of the Google Play release]. Applies to the Subber app for Android.

The short version

  • No account. No phone number, email or name is collected. The name you type goes only to friends you invite or show your QR code to.
  • Your messages, contacts, passwords and chats stay on your phone, encrypted and locked with your PIN. We have no copy and no way to get one.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no crash reporting.
  • The only thing Subber ever sends to us is your Google Play purchase receipt, to check that your subscription is active. We don’t keep it.

What stays on your phone

Your secret key, your contacts, the passwords you save, your chat history and your settings. All of it is encrypted with a key that is unlocked by your PIN together with a secret held in your phone’s secure hardware. It is not included in Android backups and is never uploaded. If you uninstall Subber or use Reset app, it is gone for good.

Photos and videos you pick to hide a message in are processed on your phone. A photo or video you have just sent stays in the app’s private storage only while that chat is open, then it is deleted.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to scan a friend’s QR code. Nothing is recorded or stored.
  • Fingerprint or face: only if you turn on biometric unlock. Your phone checks it; Subber never sees it.
  • Internet: only for the subscription check.
  • Photos and videos: Subber has no access to your gallery. You pick a single photo or video with Android’s own picker.
  • Clipboard: read only when you tap Paste. Passwords you copy are cleared from the clipboard after 60 seconds.

Children

Subber is meant for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children.

Your choices

Everything Subber knows about you is on your phone, under your control. To delete it, use Settings → Reset app, or uninstall Subber. There is nothing on our side to delete. To cancel a subscription, use Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a change affects what leaves your phone, the app will tell you before it happens.
