subbr

Privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026. subbr is made by an independent developer (“we”). This policy covers the subbr app for Android.

The short version

subbr collects no personal data, and has no way to. The app has no internet permission: it cannot connect to any server, ours or anyone else's. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

  • Your name (as your friends see it in a QR code), your friends, their passwords, optional notes and contact details, and the last 10 messages per friend. All of it is encrypted with a key kept in your phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore).
  • Messages set to self-destruct (“show for” or “open once”) are not kept.
  • subbr turns off Android backup, so none of this is copied to the cloud.
  • Delete it any time: Settings → Delete everything, or uninstall subbr.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to scan a friend's QR code, or to take a photo to hide a message in, when you choose to. Nothing is recorded otherwise.
  • Photos and videos: subbr uses Android's photo picker, so it sees only the items you pick. It has no access to your gallery.
  • Fingerprint, face or screen lock: only if you turn on the lock in Settings. Android checks it; subbr never sees your fingerprint or PIN.
  • Clipboard: read only when you tap Paste or Reveal from clipboard. What subbr copies is cleared after about a minute.

Messages you send

Hidden messages travel only through the apps you choose (for example WhatsApp, Telegram or email), and those apps' own privacy policies apply to them. Without the password, the hidden content can't be read.

Children

subbr is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
